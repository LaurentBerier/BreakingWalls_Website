A Canadian Game Sale?

Apr 7, 2025 · Written by Breaking Walls Team

Did you know that we’re in Montreal, Canada? Yes! Breaking Walls is a Canadian studio. And recently, everywhere we go, we hear about “Buying Canadian”.

Quickly, we realized that, as gamers, we didn’t have an exhaustive resource to know which games are made by Canadian studios or Canadian devs!

As we did some research, something dawned on us: so many great games are made in this country and a lot of people (us included 😮) don’t even know they are Canadian!

So here’s what we did...

The list

We created a list of video games studios and devs that are in Canada. The list tells you if they are fully Canadian studios or if they belong to a company originating from another country.

Get it here ✨

We are still updating it, so please tell us if we got anything wrong or forgot someone! 🙏

The Curator Page

We created a dedicated curator store page on Steam! Follow it to find Canadian games you would like in any genre you like!

Follow the page here!🍁

This page is also still being updated, there are SO many games that still need a review, and they are all added one by one, by one person!

Once again, if you see any mistake, please tell us!

The Group

We also have a Steam group, and we encourage all Canadian Devs, Canadians gamers and enthusiasts from around the world to join the group!

Join now!

The… Sale???

OK, so, last thing! We are currently trying to organize a Steam Sale for Canada Day. For that, we need other Canadian devs to confirm their potential participation with us.

So if you are one, please contact us! We need as many participants as possible if we want this to be possible!

Click here to fill the form

If you want this to happen as a gamer, show it to Steam!

Ask your favorite Canadian game studios and devs to get in touch with us! Share this post with as many friends as you can! Tag Steam and everyone who might be interested!

Together, we can make it happen🙌🍁
