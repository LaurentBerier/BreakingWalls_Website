  • BW Labs

BW Labs is the experimental wing of Breaking Walls, a space where we break things fast to build smarter. It's where new ideas take flight, from wild prototypes to short-form playable experiences.

Our mission is simple: explore bold gameplay concepts without the pressure of polish. BW Labs fuels innovation at Breaking Walls by testing mechanics, tones, and tech that might one day power our next big title or simply exist for the fun of it.

The Games

  • Fold & Fly

    Fold & Fly is a game about gliding a paper plane through whimsical dreamscapes, catching wind currents, threading narrow gaps, and collecting stars to raise your score. If you enjoy simple, satisfying games that mix relaxing vibes with fast-paced moments of flow, this one’s for you!

    Check it

  • Sentinel: Stone of Disruption

    Sentinel: Stones of Disruption is a pulse-pounding sci-fi roguelike shoot'em up on a remote planet. Step into the metal shoes of a powerful robot on a mission to reclaim a stolen crystal from alien adversaries. Restoring this gem is crucial to bringing harmony back to a shattered world.

    Check it

  • Go Boom!

    Create massive destruction in this completely free local multiplayer project for 2 to 4 players! This fun project was made in three days by our BW Labs team based off of a game jam challenge: destruction, and we can safely say we delivered a sweet symphony of explosions and chaos.
