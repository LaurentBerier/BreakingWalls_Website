SENTINEL: STONES OF DISRUPTION

Check it

SHOOT’EM UP

Sentinel: Stones of Disruption is an action-packed roguelike shoot'em up, featuring fast-paced combat, procedurally generated levels, and a variety of weapons and power-ups to discover. With stunning visuals, a gripping storyline, and challenging gameplay, Sentinel: Stones of Disruption is a must-play for fans of the genre.

PROVOKE CHAIN REACTIONS

Will you be able to navigate the dangerous terrain, defeat the golems, and recover the lost crystal, or will you succumb to the perils of the planet? The fate of the mission rests in your hands !

ELIMINATE HORDES

In Sentinel: Stones of Disruption, players take on the role of a highly advanced robot sent on a reconnaissance mission to a distant planet where a valuable crystal has been lost. However, upon arrival, they find themselves under attack by powerful rock golems who are determined to protect the magical crystal, which is crucial to their survival.

UPGRADE YOUR WEAPONRY

Players must use all their skills and weapons to fight off these formidable enemies and recover the lost crystal, while navigating treacherous environments filled with hazards and traps. Maximize your score by destroying everything that comes in your way !

Sentinel offers a fast paced style game-play that pushes you to think and react quick if you are to survive the hordes of enemies on your path.

After each horde, upgrade your weaponry & skill set using the tokens you’ve earned along the way.

Eliminate the numerous groups of creatures to face the final horde !
