WHO WE ARE

At Breaking Walls, we are at the forefront of innovation, pushing boundaries in the gaming industry with a focus on creating immersive and AI-driven experiences. If this sounds ambitious, that's because it is. Our expertise extends from developing the award-winning "AWAY: The Survival Series" to working on AI-powered tools. Every day, we tackle unique challenges and devise solutions that set the industry standard.

And there's more.

We take pride in the exceptional visual quality of our projects. To us, delivering an immersive experience means creating a world players can believe in. At Breaking Walls, we strive for the extraordinary. If you share this vision, then we're eager to collaborate.

 

HOW WE WORK

To meet our high standards, we provide our team with the autonomy, tools, and support they need to excel. Let's be clear: life at Breaking Walls is exhilarating.

We're on the cutting edge of gaming technology, setting trends, and creating groundbreaking experiences.

This journey is not for the faint-hearted; it demands passion, commitment, and an insatiable curiosity for what's next.

 

WHY WE WANT YOU

We want you on the team because you relish challenges. Because you think outside the box. Because you hate the status quo.

We want you because you have the talent it takes to succeed, and the ambition to figure things out when talent isn't enough.

We especially want you because you want to collaborate with the best in the industry to create something truly spectacular.

 

HOW TO APPLY

Email us at careers@breakingwalls.co! While we'd love to see your CV, we also want to know more about you as a person. Breaking Walls isn't some mythical entity, we're just people like you who want to create cool stuff, so please don't hesitate to tell us more about yourself!
