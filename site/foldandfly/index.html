Soar. Glide. Explore. Rediscover the joy of flying through whimsical landscapes, dynamic skies, and handcrafted dioramas.

🌟 What is Fold & Fly?

Fold & Fly is a cozy, replayable flying game where you launch and steer a paper plane across mesmerizing paper worlds. Ride the wind, dive through narrow gaps, collect stars, and uncover glowing portals that lead to new dimensions.

Whether you want to relax or chase high scores, Fold & Fly is the perfect escape.

🛠️ Features

  • 🌬️ Intuitive flight controls – Easy to learn, hard to master

  • 🌄 Procedurally generated World – Infinite maps with each run

  • 🌩️ Dynamic weather – From gentle breezes to thunderstorms and snow

  • 💫 Portal system – Find glowing origami to teleport and double your score

  • 🪁 Whimsical art direction – Cardboard cliffs, floating islands, and dreamy skies and more

  • 🧠 Built for flow – Fast restarts, satisfying combos, and pure motion joy

  • 🇨🇦 Crafted in Canada – By a small indie team from Montréal
