BUILD YOUR ADVENTURE

Venus: The Last Ascent is a survival base-building adventure set on the hostile surface of Venus. Humanity's last hope lies above the clouds in Cloud City, a distant sanctuary that can only be reached by crossing one of the most dangerous worlds in the solar system.

Build and expand a massive fortress on wheels, turning your base into a self-sustaining mobile stronghold. Mine valuable resources, manage power, manufacture equipment, and defend your reactors against relentless robotic threats and the planet's unforgiving environment.

Customize your fortress with advanced machinery, automated systems, and powerful defenses. Explore a vast and deadly landscape, uncover lost technologies, and make strategic decisions that will determine whether your expedition survives the journey.

Combining physics-driven construction, exploration, automation, and survival gameplay, Venus: The Last Ascent challenges players to adapt, innovate, and keep moving toward humanity's final destination in the skies.
