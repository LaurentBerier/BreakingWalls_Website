Breaking Walls Privacy Policy

At Breaking Walls, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle personal information in relation to our video game, which is distributed on various gaming platforms, including Steam, PlayStation Network, Xbox Live, Epic Games Store, and other platforms as they become available (collectively, "Platforms"). 

1. Information We Collect 

  • Through Our Video Game: We do not collect, store, or have access to any personal information from players directly through our video game. The game does not communicate with us or transmit any user data to our servers. 

  • Through Our Website and Social Media: The only personal information we may receive is if you voluntarily provide it to us, such as when you:  

  • Register on our website for a mailing list (e.g., providing your email address). 

  • Follow us or interact with us on social media platforms (e.g., your public profile information, depending on your privacy settings on those platforms). 

2. How We Use Information 

  • We Do Not Use Information from the Game: We do not use any information collected from our video game for tracking, advertising, or any other purposes. 

  • Mailing List: If you register for our mailing list, we will use your email address to send you information about our company, products, or services, such as news about new releases, updates, or special offers. You can unsubscribe from our mailing list at any time. 

  • Social Media: We use social media to engage with our players and build our community. We may use information you publicly share on social media to respond to your comments or questions. 

3. Third-Party Services 

  • Gaming Platforms: Our video game may include features provided by the Platforms, such as trophies, achievements, or online leaderboards. These features are subject to the Platforms' own privacy policies, and we do not have access to or control over any personal information collected by them. 

  • Other Third Parties: We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any other third parties, except as required by law or to comply with a legal process. 

4. Data Security 

  • We take reasonable measures to protect the security of any personal information you provide to us through our website or social media channels, including using appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. 

5. Children's Privacy 

  • Our video game is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete such information. 

6. Your Privacy Rights 

  • You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information that we hold. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below. 

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy 

  • We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting the revised policy on our website. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates or changes. 

8. Contact Us 

  • If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at info@breakingwalls.co. 

By playing our video game or interacting with us through our website or social media, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
