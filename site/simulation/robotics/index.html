From capture to dataset

Capture

Record full-body movement and hand poses for the tasks of interest.

Validation

Combine automated checks with human review, and recapture where needed.

Delivery

Prepare labeled, synchronized files and retarget data to the target skeleton as required.

Data and formats

Motion

Skeletal motion in BVH or FBX, hand poses, joint rotations and velocities.

Synchronized exports

Time-aligned camera streams, RGB, depth and point clouds depending on the capture setup.

Metadata

Task segments, timestamps, session tags and quality flags.

Research applications

Imitation learning, reinforcement learning pretraining, sim-to-real research and industrial tasks such as assembly, tool use and object handling.
